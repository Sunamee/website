About Us

A team built around your growth.

Sunamee is a team of strategists, operators and relationship builders who have spent their careers helping brands grow.

We started Sunamee because the best opportunities rarely sit inside one discipline. A product launch can depend on the right athlete partner. A wellness brand can live or die on its digital experience. We bring those pieces together under one vision.

Every client works with a dedicated team that stays with them from strategy through execution. We keep our client list small on purpose, so each partnership gets senior attention and real follow-through.

Collaborative
We work as an extension of your team, not as outside advisors who hand off a report.
Connected
Our network spans brands, sports, wellness and technology, and we put it to work for you.
Accountable
We agree on clear goals up front and measure our work against them.
Long-term
We build partnerships meant to last well beyond the first project.
